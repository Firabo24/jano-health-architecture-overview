# Current project status and claims boundary

**Status date:** 5 November 2026

Jano Health is in an early-stage architecture and prototype phase. The Project Founder approved the consolidated architecture baseline on 5 November 2026 for architecture scope only. The approved design can guide controlled engineering and test preparation. It has not thereby received independent design-authority, clinical, security, privacy/legal, partner, facility, verification or release approval.

## What we can say

- The Project Founder approved the architecture baseline, including its five logical responsibility areas and core design boundaries.
- The architecture has explicit design concerns for offline-aware operation, localization, interoperability, privacy, security and governed AI.
- A prototype exists for demonstration; it must not be represented as a clinically validated or production care system.
- Clinical, legal/privacy, independent security, partner, facility, usability, interoperability, operational and release validation remain separate requirements.

## What we do not claim

- No live-patient deployment or clinical-use authorization is asserted here.
- No clinical effectiveness or safety validation is asserted.
- No regulatory certification or legal compliance opinion is asserted.
- No Ministry of Health, NDHx, Fayda or other external partner integration/authorization is asserted.
- No production SLA, operational adoption, revenue, or scale metrics are asserted.

Future public status changes should be supported by verifiable evidence and approved wording.
