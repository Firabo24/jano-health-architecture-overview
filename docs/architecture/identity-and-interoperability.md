# Identity and interoperability — public design posture

Jano Health is intended to work within Ethiopia's wider digital-health ecosystem and, where authorized, other health and identity ecosystems.

## Identity posture

National identity systems are external authorities. Jano's architecture is intended to maintain stable internal person and patient references, rather than making an external identifier the primary key for clinical records. Patient matching, identity correction, authentication, workforce affiliation and access authorization are separate matters that require detailed governance.

Fayda is an intended foundational external identity anchor in the Jano identity direction. This statement does **not** mean Jano has a relying-party agreement, credentials, partner access, production integration, or approval to retrieve or process Fayda attributes. Any integration is subject to current law, approved purpose, partner onboarding, consent/legal-basis requirements and technical conformance.

## Interoperability posture

External health-data exchange must be purpose-limited, authorized, contract-governed and testable. Jano does not claim a live connection to a national exchange or any government platform through this public design description.

## What this page deliberately omits

It does not publish identity schemas, matching algorithms, authorization rules, security controls, trust material, message replay behavior, partner credentials, or deployment configurations. Those belong in controlled engineering and assurance documentation.
