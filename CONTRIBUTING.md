# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Jano Health's public architecture overview.

## Suitable contributions

- Correct factual or editorial errors in public documentation.
- Suggest improvements to clarity, accessibility and terminology.
- Identify public references to relevant standards or ecosystem resources.

## Do not submit

- Patient data, screenshots of real records, personal identifiers or clinical exports.
- Credentials, tokens, keys, private partner documents or confidential correspondence.
- Detailed vulnerability reports or attack paths in public issues. Follow `SECURITY.md` instead.
- Clinical protocols or treatment recommendations presented as approved policy.

## Review

Public documentation changes are reviewed by maintainers. A merged documentation change does not establish clinical, legal, security, partner or release approval. Do not infer formal approval from repository status or GitHub checks.
